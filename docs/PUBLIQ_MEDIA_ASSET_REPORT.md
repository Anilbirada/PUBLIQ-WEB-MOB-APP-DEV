## 1. Executive Summary & Media Analysis

### Strict Rebranding & Copyright Protection Mandate
In strict accordance with the project guidelines:
1. **Zero Source Branding Exposure:** The source restaurant's name ("Qissa"), wordmark, circular floral logo, custom blossom branding, and signature hippo glassware are strictly excluded from all customer and admin screens, source code, database tables, alt text, and URLs.
2. **Exclusively PUBLIQ BAR & RESTAURANT:** Every touchpoint in the web application uses PUBLIQ's original luxury brand identity, antique gold crest monogram, midnight navy / obsidian palette, and editorial typography.
3. **Rights & Licensing Compliance:** Publicly hosted assets on third-party restaurant domains remain the intellectual property of their respective creators. No unauthorized third-party images or logos are hotlinked or redistributed. All integrated images in PUBLIQ are organized under an authorized, optimized local asset structure (`frontend/public/images/publiq/`) with neutral, SEO-optimized descriptive filenames.

## 4. Video Availability Audit & Strategy

- **Audit Confirmation:** As documented on Page 8 of the PDF reference, **zero playable MP4 or WebM video files** or embedded iframe players were surfaced from the source website. The gallery merely pointed visitors to an external Instagram handle.
- **PUBLIQ Video Implementation:** To deliver the requested cinematic feel, the PUBLIQ platform integrates **six dedicated high-definition video assets (1280x720, H.264 MP4)** stored locally in `frontend/public/videos/`:
  1. `hero.mp4` — Full-screen ambient restaurant arrival, warm lights & cocktail pour.
  2. `ambience.mp4` — Dining hall, candlelit tables & architectural features.
  3. `bar.mp4` — Mixology bar counter, ice-carving & spirit bottles.
  4. `cocktails.mp4` — Signature smoking elixir and cocktail craft.
  5. `food.mp4` — Charcoal tandoor grill, butter basting & sizzling plates.
  6. `party.mp4` — Evening celebrations and nightlife atmosphere.

All video sections feature graceful fallback posters (`poster="/images/publiq/interior/publiq-interior-hero.webp"`), reduced-motion checks, and muted autoplay loops.
